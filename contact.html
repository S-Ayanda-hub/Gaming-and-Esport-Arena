Frequently Asked Questions

Any hardware specifications do you offer?

Every rig runs an RTX 4090, Ryzen 9 CPU, 32GB RAM, and a 240Hz+ monitor, alongside a full peripheral wall to choose from.

Can I bring my own gaming peripherals?

Yes — mice, keyboards, and headsets are welcome. Controllers for console booths must be checked in with staff first.

Do you host private birthday parties?

Absolutely. Our Birthday Party Package reserves a private section of the arena with multiplayer setups for up to 20 guests.

How does the VIP membership system

VIP members get priority booking, a discounted hourly rate, and access to exclusive tournament seeding events.
